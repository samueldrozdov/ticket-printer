# Hardware

## Computer (Raspberry Pi 4B)

The Raspberry Pi 4B controls the printer and receives audio from the microphone.

- **Power:** Requires a 5 V / 3 A USB-C power supply plugged into a wall outlet.
- **Battery:** No built-in battery; must stay connected to power while running.
- **Printer connection:** Bluetooth, with no data cable required.
- **Microphone connection:** USB.
- **Network connection:** Wi-Fi or Ethernet for access from other devices.
- **Where to purchase:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C8LV6VNZ).
- **Reference:** [Raspberry Pi specifications](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rpi4/raspberry-pi-4-product-brief.pdf).

## Printer (Netum NT-1809)

The Netum NT-1809 thermal printer prints text and images on 58 mm thermal receipt paper.

- **Power:** Runs on a rechargeable battery.
- **Charging:** Connect its charging cable to a 5 V / 2 A power supply plugged into a wall outlet.
- **Unplugged use:** Can print without a power cable while the battery has charge.
- **Connection:** Bluetooth to the Raspberry Pi while printing; no data cable is needed for the wireless setup.
- **Wired option:** USB interface.
- **Where to purchase:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0919HGLSH).
- **Reference:** [NETUM specifications](https://www.netum.net/products/nt-1809-thermal-printer).

### Receipt paper (58 mm thermal)

58 mm thermal receipt paper provides the surface for printed text and images.

- **Power:** None.
- **Placement:** Load the roll inside the printer's paper compartment.
- **Connections:** No cables or electronic connection needed.
- **Where to purchase:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08RRZT2JL).

## Microphone (Samson Go Mic)

The Samson Go Mic is a portable USB condenser microphone that captures voice and other audio.

- **Power:** Draws power over USB; no separate wall adapter or batteries needed.
- **Connection:** Plug into a Raspberry Pi USB port using its USB cable. The same cable carries power and audio.
- **During use:** Must stay plugged in while recording.
- **Alternative connection:** Can plug into a computer instead if recording there.
- **Where to purchase:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001R76D42).
- **Reference:** [Samson connection instructions](https://storage.googleapis.com/samson-production/uploads/documents/GoMic_OM_EN_V2.3.pdf).

## Setup peripherals

### Monitor (HDMI)

An HDMI monitor displays the Raspberry Pi's desktop during setup or local use.

- **Power:** Uses its own power supply, typically plugged into a wall outlet. HDMI does not power the monitor.
- **Connection:** Connect its HDMI input to a Raspberry Pi micro-HDMI output using a micro-HDMI-to-HDMI cable.
- **After setup:** Can be disconnected if the Pi is managed remotely.

### Keyboard and mouse (USB or Bluetooth)

Provide input when setting up or using the Raspberry Pi locally.

- **Power:** Wired models draw power from the Pi over USB. Wireless models use replaceable or rechargeable batteries, depending on the model.
- **Connection:** Plug wired devices or a wireless USB receiver into the Pi's USB ports, or pair Bluetooth models directly with the Pi.
- **After setup:** Can be disconnected if the Pi is managed remotely.
